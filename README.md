## The entry

> Hero. You made it — past the moat I never filled, up the stairs I never mended. Sit. The kettle's on; the good cups are already out. I had a plan once: banners, eclipses, a throne of teeth. Then came the ledgers, the goblins' union — fairly won, mind — and the damp, and my knees. So take the amulet. Take the crown. Take the whole kingdom; I signed it over at breakfast. Only, before you swing that sword — reach the top shelf for me. My tea's up there. I've earned one quiet cup. Stay a while.
